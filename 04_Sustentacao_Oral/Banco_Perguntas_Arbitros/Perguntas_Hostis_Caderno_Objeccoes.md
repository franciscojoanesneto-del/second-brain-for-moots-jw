# ⚔️ Banco de Perguntas Hostis & Caderno de Objeções (FDI Moot)

## Pergunta 1 (Presidente Rigoroso — Jurisdição & Treaty Shopping):
> *"Counsel Walker, isn't it true that your client restructured its corporate chain mere months before the dispute formalized, solely to gain access to this Tribunal? How is that not an egregious abuse of the ICSID system under Phoenix Action?"*

### Resposta Padrão Ouro (Julia Walker):
> *"Mr. President, with utmost respect, the timing of the corporate reorganization in Phoenix Action occurred after the dispute had already crystallized into actual litigation. In our case, the restructuring was undertaken for legitimate commercial hedging in the ordinary course of business, long before any regulatory measures were announced. The award in Tidewater v. Venezuela explicitly establishes that prospective restructuring before the emergence of a specific dispute is legitimate corporate planning, not treaty abuse."*

## Pergunta 2 (Co-árbitro Técnico — FET & Police Powers):
> *"Counsel, are you suggesting that a sovereign State is forever handcuffed to a subsidized tariff regime, even when facing severe fiscal imbalance? Does international law really guarantee a frozen regulatory environment?"*

### Resposta Padrão Ouro (Julia Walker):
> *"Co-Arbitrator, Claimant does not claim a right to a frozen legal order. What international law unequivocally prohibits — as held in Charanne and Isolux — is the total evisceration of the essential economic framework upon which specific and deliberate state commitments were made to induce our investment. When a State promises stability to attract hundreds of millions of dollars, it cannot subsequently demolish that very foundation without fair compensation."*
