# 🎙️ Roteiro Mestre de Sustentação Oral (FDI Moot) — Julia Azevedo Walker

## ⏱️ Minuto 0:00 - 1:30 | Abertura Forense & Roadmap
- *"Mr. President, distinguished Members of the Arbitral Tribunal. My name is Julia Azevedo Walker, and it is my distinct honor to represent the Claimant, Vesta Energy Holdings, in today's proceedings."*
- *"Today, this Tribunal is called upon to decide whether the sovereign word of a State, solemnly pledged in an international investment treaty, retains its binding sanctity, or whether it may be unilaterally discarded under the convenient banner of regulatory discretion."*
- *"I will address two decisive issues: First, that this Tribunal exercises uncontested jurisdiction under Article 25 of the ICSID Convention; and Second, that Respondent's measures breached the standard of Fair and Equitable Treatment under Article 3 of the BIT."*

## ⏱️ Minuto 1:30 - 6:30 | Issue 1: Jurisdição e Competência
- Demonstração do cumprimento dos 4 pilares do *Salini Test*.
- Rejeição contundente da alegação de treaty shopping (*Tidewater v. Venezuela* distinguishing).

## ⏱️ Minuto 6:30 - 13:00 | Issue 2: Mérito & Violação do FET
- Destruição das legítimas expectativas pactuadas no regime de 2018.
- Violação de *due process* e proporcionalidade (*Tecmed*, *Biwater Gauff*).

## ⏱️ Minuto 13:00 - 14:00 | Conclusão & Prayer for Relief
- *"For these reasons, Claimant respectfully requests this Tribunal to adjudge and declare that Respondent has breached its treaty obligations and to award full reparation for the damages sustained. Thank you, Members of the Tribunal. I now reserve my remaining time for rebuttal."*
