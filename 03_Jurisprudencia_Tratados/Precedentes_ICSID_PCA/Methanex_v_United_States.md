# 🏛️ Methanex Corporation v. United States of America (UNCITRAL 2005)

## Ratio Decidendi
Consagração máxima da Doutrina do Poder de Polícia:
*"As a matter of general international law, a non-discriminatory regulation for a public purpose, which is enacted in accordance with due process and, which affects, inter alios, a foreign investor or investment is not deemed expropriatory and compensable..."*
